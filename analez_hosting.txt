18. ASOSIY BOTTLENECKLAR

1) PostgreSQL discovery query: Haversine hisoblash va katta candidate setni sort qilish.
   50k/10 VU’da p95 4.7 s va throughput collapse kuzatildi.
2) Polling handle_as_tasks=False: Telegram tarmog‘idagi sekin send/getChat navbatdagi update’ni
   kutdiradi. Bu correctness uchun sodda, throughput uchun cheklov.
3) Bitta process va 5+5 DB connection pool. Hozir Redis emas, PostgreSQL CPU/query asosiy
   bottleneck; Redis p95 0.090 ms.
4) Tashqi Telegram rate limitlari lokal service-layer load testda yo‘q. Real throughput undan
   pastroq bo‘lishi mumkin.
5) Outbox/rate limit observability yetishmaydi; incidentda notification va flood boshqaruvi
   qo‘lda monitoring talab qiladi.
